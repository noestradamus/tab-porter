# Tab Porter Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

Tab Porter organizes Chrome tabs using rules you choose. It processes tab information on your device and sends no user data to the developer, advertisers, or other third parties.

## What the extension uses

- **Open tabs.** Tab Porter reads tab URLs and page titles to find matches for your rules. It checks which tab is selected so it can wait 30 seconds after you switch away before grouping that tab.
- **Your rules.** Chrome stores your group names, colors, and matching rules in the profile where you use Tab Porter. Your rules can include domains, URL paths, title phrases, publisher names, GitHub repositories, folders, filenames, and YouTube video types you choose. Rules stay there until you edit or delete them, or uninstall the extension.
- **Recently moved and Undo.** For up to five recent moves, Tab Porter keeps the tab title or hostname, group details, time of the move, and identifiers for the tab, windows, and neighboring tabs. These help you find a tab or return it to its previous position. Chrome also holds temporary tab selections, countdowns, and instructions to skip tabs you manually ungroup or return with Undo. Restarting Chrome clears this session data.

## Your controls

Open Tab Porter and choose **Manage groups** to change or delete a rule. To remove a tab group that already exists, use Chrome’s tab-group menu. If you sync history and tabs with your Google Account, Chrome can sync those native groups across your devices. [Chrome’s tab-group settings](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/2391819?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop)

## Limited use and contact

Tab Porter uses browsing data only to organize your tabs, show recent moves, and let you undo them. Its use of this data follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. [Chrome Web Store Limited Use policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/limited-use)

For privacy questions, use the developer contact on Tab Porter’s Chrome Web Store listing.
